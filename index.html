03 / STAYS ON YOUR MACHINE

Your logs.
Your machine.
Your business.

AigentIntel works offline. It makes no network requests and does not upload logs, prompts, or responses.

✓

No keys. No account.

Reads existing logs without modifying them. Preferences live in a local settings file.

✓

Careful with what it reads.

Filters terminal control characters, validates metadata and numeric values, and applies resource limits.

✓

Limitations stay visible.

Reports are marked incomplete when resource limits exclude usage. Review exported reports before sharing: they may include project paths and usage totals.
